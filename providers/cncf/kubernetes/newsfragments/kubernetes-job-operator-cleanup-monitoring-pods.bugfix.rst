Fix monitoring pod leak in ``KubernetesJobOperator``: ``execute()``, ``execute_complete()`` and ``on_kill()`` now clean up the monitoring/log-streaming pods discovered via ``get_pods()``. These pods have no ``ownerReferences`` and were not reaped by the Job's ``ttl_seconds_after_finished`` or by the ``Foreground`` cascade on ``on_kill``. The inherited ``on_finish_action`` parameter (``delete_pod`` / ``delete_succeeded_pod`` / ``keep_pod``) is now honoured for ``KubernetesJobOperator``, matching the documented behaviour of ``KubernetesPodOperator``. Per-pod cleanup errors are logged but never mask a Job-level failure.
